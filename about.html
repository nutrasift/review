About NutraSift

Sifting through the noise to bring you honest, evidence-based health and supplement reviews.

Our Mission

At NutraSift, our mission is to empower consumers with factual, transparent, and clinically verified information about dietary supplements, wellness products, and health formulas. The supplement market is flooded with marketing hype, misleading claims, and hidden proprietary blends. We act as your independent filter, analyzing raw ingredients, manufacturer certifications, scientific literature, and real user consensus before you spend your hard-earned money.

💡 Our Core Guarantee: We never accept payment from supplement vendors in exchange for positive reviews. Our evaluations are strictly based on ingredient dosages, clinical research, manufacturing standards (GMP & FDA-registered facilities), and consumer safety.

Our 4-Step Review Methodology

Every product reviewed on NutraSift undergoes a rigorous 4-step evaluation process designed to enforce high E-E-A-T (Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, and Trustworthiness) standards:

  • 1. Ingredient & Dosage Verification: We cross-examine every active ingredient against peer-reviewed studies published in PubMed, BioMed, and clinical journals to determine if the formula contains effective dosages.
  • 2. Manufacturing Quality Audit: We verify whether the formula is manufactured in an FDA-registered, cGMP (Current Good Manufacturing Practice) certified facility in the USA or accredited global labs.
  • 3. Safety & YMYL Compliance: We flag potential side effects, allergen risks, drug interactions, and strict usage guidelines for pregnant women, nursing mothers, or individuals taking prescription medication.
  • 4. Real Consumer Consensus: We aggregate verified buyer feedback across multiple non-sponsored channels to identify common results, realistic timelines, and refund satisfaction rates.

Editorial Standards & Ethics

NutraSift adheres to strict editorial guidelines. We strictly comply with FDA and FTC regulations regarding health claims and affiliate disclosures. We do not claim any supplement can "cure," "treat," or "prevent" medical conditions. All medical claims are framed with scientific caution, advising readers to consult a certified healthcare professional before starting any new wellness regimen.
